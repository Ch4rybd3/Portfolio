Remora

A DFIR case management platform built to cover the full investigative lifecycle. Manage cases, follow structured playbooks, process and tag artifacts, maintain chain of custody, take structured notes, and generate reports automatically — all from one place.

Designed from first principles to match how analysts actually work: fast triage, flexible note-taking, and deep artifact integration without forcing you out of your terminal.

Building Remora because nothing out there truly integrates the full DFIR workflow — from acquisition to final report — without forcing context switches between 5 different tools.

// CONFERENCE TALK · WEB À QUÉBEC 2023 Delivered

"Phishing and Social Engineering —
Ever been in a hacker's mind?"

A walk through the mind of an attacker — from target selection to payload delivery. Rather than a classic awareness lecture, I placed the audience directly inside the attacker's perspective using reverse psychology, making the threat viscerally real rather than abstract.

The talk covered the full phishing kill chain: initial recon, pretexting, email spoofing and infrastructure, payload delivery, evasion, and post-click actions. I demonstrated live tooling (GoPhish, Evilginx, custom pretexts) in a controlled setup. Strong audience engagement and discussions continued well past the session slot.

The goal was to make people uncomfortable in a productive way. Not "here are phishing red flags" but "here is exactly how I would phish you, step by step, right now." Reaction was exactly what I hoped for.

Phishing Social Engineering Security Awareness Red Team GoPhish Evilginx
Québec City, Canada WAQ 2023 · April 2023 ~150 attendees French
